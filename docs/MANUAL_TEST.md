# Real-device touch checklist

Use the live cabinet: https://rom-bar.github.io/gimbot/ . Emulation can verify layout and input events, but it cannot prove physical touch feel, haptic support, browser zoom behavior, or thumb reach. Try on at least one phone; leave a box checked only after testing it yourself.

## Portrait

- [ ] At 320–360 px wide, the board and controls fit without page scrolling or clipped text.
- [ ] Left, Right and Down are comfortable to reach with the left thumb; A/B are distinct under the right thumb.
- [ ] Hold Left/Right for a moment: the piece waits briefly, then moves repeatedly at a comfortable speed.
- [ ] Hold Down: soft drop feels faster than normal falling.
- [ ] Tap A to rotate and B to hard-drop. Try holding Left while tapping A.
- [ ] Open Controls, switch to Gestures, and try a horizontal swipe, downward swipe and tap. Return to Buttons mode afterward if preferred.
- [ ] Try Small and Large button sizes, left-handed mode, opacity, haptics, CRT and sound settings; confirm preferences remain after reopening the game.
- [ ] Long-press a control: no browser context menu appears. Try scrolling or double-tapping outside the game; the rest of the page remains accessible.

## Block Stack

- [ ] The next-piece preview is clear and the landing ghost matches where the shape will settle.
- [ ] Enable Hold piece slot; use H to save/swap a shape once per falling piece.
- [ ] After a piece lands, slide or rotate it briefly before it locks.

## Landscape and keyboard

- [ ] Rotate the phone: the board sits between the left D-pad and right action buttons; the browser gesture bar/notch does not cover them.
- [ ] On desktop, verify keyboard controls still work (arrows/WASD, Space/A, P/Esc, R) and focus indicators are visible.
